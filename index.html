A Walk Through the Mountains

First Light

It began before sunrise. Fog still hung low over the pines and the whole valley was silent except for the crunch of my boots on the trail. The rising sun turned the mist to gold, and somewhere far ahead was the peak I had come to reach. I pulled my jacket tighter and started walking.

The Lake

About an hour in, the trail opened onto a still mountain lake. The water was so calm it held a perfect mirror of the ridge and the forest behind it. I sat on the shore for a while, caught my breath, and watched the reflection barely move. It was the kind of quiet you can only find far from the city.

Above the Clouds

The last stretch was the hardest. The trees fell away completely and I stepped out onto a bare cliff, right as the sky began to burn orange. Below me was nothing but a rolling sea of clouds. Standing on that edge, above everything, I felt small and enormous at the same time.

Nightfall

By the time I reached the ridge above town, night had fallen and the lights below had all flickered on, spread out like stars under the fading purple sky. I was exhausted, but it was the good kind of tired — the kind that comes from a day well spent.
